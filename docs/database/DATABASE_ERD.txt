═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INDEXED COLUMNS (Primary Lookup Paths):

Auth Layer:
  ⭐ users.email (case-insensitive)
  ⭐ user_sessions.token
  ⭐ user_sessions(is_active, expires_at)

Company Data:
  ⭐ companies.email_address
  ⭐ companies.quickbooks_id
  ⭐ company_contacts(company_id)

Sales:
  ⭐ sales(company_id, status)
  ⭐ sales(sales_rep_id, sale_date DESC)
  ⭐ opportunities(status)

Financial:
  ⭐ invoices(status, due_date)
  ⭐ invoices(company_id, created_at DESC)
